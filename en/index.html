Frequently asked questions

Who is the programme for?

High-volume private investors, family offices and corporate accounts. Admission is by invitation or after an eligibility review following the introductory call.

What is the portfolio threshold?

The threshold is set periodically according to programme capacity and shared during the introductory call. The portfolio range in the form only routes you to the right manager.

How is pricing structured?

Volume-tiered, written and transparent. There are no hidden fees; all terms are provided in writing before any agreement.

Is any return guaranteed?

No. Leveraged products carry a high level of risk; no return is guaranteed and past performance is not indicative of future results. The programme offers preferential terms and service, not outcomes.

How is my personal data protected?

It is processed under Turkish data-protection law (KVKK) solely to evaluate your request and contact you; it is not shared with third parties for marketing. See the legal notice.

How does the booking process work?

Fill in the form or message us on WhatsApp; your manager calls within 24 hours. A 20-minute introductory call covers goals and eligibility; if suitable, you receive a personal programme plan and written risk disclosure.
