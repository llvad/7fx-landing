1. Risk disclosure — leveraged trading

Important: Leveraged trading carries a high level of risk and is not suitable for every investor. You may lose all of your deposited margin and, in some circumstances, more. Do not trade if you do not fully understand these risks; seek independent advice.
  • Leverage magnifies both gains and losses; a small price move can wipe out your margin.
  • Margin calls: if your margin falls below the required level you may be asked to deposit more; otherwise positions may be closed without further notice and any loss is yours.
  • Market, liquidity and currency risk: prices can move sharply; some instruments may become illiquid and spreads may widen; trading in a currency other than your account currency exposes you to exchange-rate movements.
  • Gaps and orders: stop-loss orders may be executed at the first available price, not the price you set; they do not guarantee a limited loss.
  • Systems: connectivity, hardware or software failures can delay or prevent order execution.
  • Counterparty and custody: ask for, and review in writing, the regulatory status of the counterparty and how client assets are held before opening an account.
  • Nature of information: market notes, analyses and conversations with your manager are general information, not personal investment advice, portfolio management or investment consultancy. No return is guaranteed; past performance is not indicative of future results.
